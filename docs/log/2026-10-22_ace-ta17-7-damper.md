# 2026-10-22 — The ACE TA17-7 profile damper is in the library

**Role(s):** mechanical engineering, purchasing, legal

## What changed

ACE Stoßdämpfer GmbH is a new brand in the library, with one family for its TUBUS TA profile
dampers:

| Family | Part number | What it is | CAD |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ta-damper` | TA17-7 | Elastomer end stop, 17 mm across and 16 mm long, 7 mm stroke, M4 stud 4 mm long | ACE STEP file, with one mounting frame |

The ACE operating and mounting instructions for the whole TUBUS TA series are in the library too.
They give every size and the limits on page 3 and page 4. The FreeCAD file has one mounting frame,
`IF_mount`, at the centre of the back face. Its z axis runs out of the impact face.

## Why it matters

The compact stage in aqtuator uses four of these dampers as end stops on its carriage. A machine
can now place the damper on a frame, and the BOM gets its data from this row.

## Next Steps

- ACE does not allow sharing. Item 6.8 of the ACE delivery and payment terms keeps all rights to
  drawings and documents for ACE, and the CAD download page shows no licence. The files are shared
  until ACE objects, the same as for HIWIN and LAMOTION. Niels Bosmans approves this in the pull
  request. The check shows a warning for it, as expected.
- Ask ACE for written permission, if the dampers stay in the design.

<details>
<summary>Technical notes</summary>

- The STEP file came from the ACE CAD download (made by CADClick) on 2026-10-22 as
  `TA17_7_2.stp`, and is stored as `cad/original/TA17-7.step`.
- The mass in the row is 4 g, from the manual. The ACE web shop page says 6 g.
- `doqs wrap` was not used to build the wrapper. Through the open FreeCAD window it crashed
  FreeCAD, because it runs the macro on a background thread. Its macro also stops with "Cannot
  add a group object to itself" on this STEP file: the importer makes its own Part container, and
  the macro counts that container's coordinate axes as extra top objects. The wrapper was built
  with the same steps by hand: `ImportGui.insert`, the importer's Part container relabelled
  `TA17-7`, `cad_build.frame()`, `cad_build.show_new_objects()`, then the frame turned and saved.
- Evidence in stoq-private: `evidence/ace/2026-10-22_terms-and-conditions.pdf`.
