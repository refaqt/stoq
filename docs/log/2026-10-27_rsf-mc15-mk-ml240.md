# 2026-10-27 — The RSF MC 15 MK scale with a 240 mm measuring length is in the library

**Role(s):** mechanical engineering, purchasing

## What changed

The library now has a second length of the RSF MC 15 MK steel tape scale: MB-MC15-MK-ML240, with a
240 mm measuring length on a tape 260 mm long. The 220 mm scale stays in the library, unchanged.

The 240 mm scale is our own model, built in the same way as the 220 mm one, from the same RSF
drawing. A comparison with the RSF 100 mm file passed for every size that file can show. The
start and the end of the measuring length cannot be compared with any RSF scale file. They were
only checked with the RSF read head file, so they are marked as not confirmed.

## Why it matters

In the aqtuator compact stage, the carriage can move 236 mm when both dampers are fully pressed.
A 220 mm scale is too short for that. A 240 mm scale covers the full movement, with 2 mm to spare
at each end. The compact stage will switch to the 240 mm scale.

## Next Steps

- Switch the compact stage in aqtuator to MB-MC15-MK-ML240. That is a separate change in the
  aqtuator repository.
- Order the scale as MC 15 MK with ML 240 mm.
- The open points of the 220 mm work stay open: the ID number of the 0.5 m read head cable, and
  written permission from RSF to share its files.

<details>
<summary>Technical notes</summary>

- Only ML changes, from 220 to 240. The tape is OL = ML + 20 = 260 mm long. The position sensor
  runs from X = 9.2 to X = 249.2 mm on the tape. All other sizes are from drawing D1158017-04 and
  are the same as for ML220.
- Mass: 0.26 m x 17 g/m = 4.4 g, written as 4 g.
- The model was built inside the FreeCAD window, as the mistake note of 2026-10-20 asks. No
  document was open, so the new document was first created and saved to its final path, and then
  the build script ran in it. The build helper does not save an open document, so it was saved by
  hand and the fingerprint was written again from the saved file. The saved file holds the screen
  settings, and every Part, Body and frame is visible.
- Comparison with `MB_MC15MK_ML100.step` from stoq-private: length 120 mm (= 100 + 20), total
  thickness 0.45 mm, steel tape 8 mm wide and 0.25 mm thick, adhesive tape 6 mm wide and 0.2 mm
  thick, both tapes centred and equally long, volume equal to the volume our layer sizes give.
- Fit test with the RSF read head part `AK-MC15-B-0.5M-SUBD15`: with its frame `IF_scale` on
  `IF_ml_start`, the head side is 7 mm before the tape start. Between `IF_ml_start` and
  `IF_ml_end` the head moves 240 mm. The head does not touch the tape in either position.
- The vendor index lists the same two RSF documents for both part numbers, with the same
  checksums.

</details>
